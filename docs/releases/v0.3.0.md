**Nodqora now signs people in against your own OIDC identity provider**, and everyone who signs in sees every environment in the install.

### Upgrading from `0.2.0`

**Back up your database first.** `0.3.0` runs a schema migration, and migrations are one-way.

- **Nodqora serves no data until you declare `nodqora.authentication`.** Every earlier release served everything to anyone who could reach it. `0.3.0` boots to *No identity provider is configured* until `./config/application.yaml` names a provider or says `none`. `none` behaves as `0.2.0` did. [Signing in](https://github.com/nodqora/nodqora/blob/v0.3.0/docs/install.md#signing-in) covers both. Add the key, then run `docker compose up -d`.
- **The `compose.yaml` asset publishes the port on loopback only.** The old one published it on every interface. If you open Nodqora from another machine, see [It listens on this machine only](https://github.com/nodqora/nodqora/blob/v0.3.0/docs/install.md#it-listens-on-this-machine-only).
